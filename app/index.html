Roadmap to the Platinum — about 200 hours

Difficulty 4 out of 10: long, not hard. No online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals. One playthrough is enough — if you protect the one missable trophy in Stage 1.

Stage 1 — Play the story 60–80 hours

Finish all five chapters with Jason and Lucia. The 12 story trophies and the final-mission Gold pop as you go. Before you start, read the missable warning below, and keep a manual save at the start of every chapter plus one from just before the final mission — there is no mission replay and no New Game Plus. Never use cheats on your trophy save: cheats switch trophies and saving off.

Trophies: the 12 story trophies + You Gotta Know When to Get Off (Gold).

Stage 2 — Reach 100% completion about 100 hours

After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion — the checklist only appears after the story, but work you did earlier still counts. You do not need everything in the game, only the checklist's numbers: 100 optional tasks; all 39 gang operations, 22 bank jobs, 7 payday loan jobs and 8 bank vaults; 15 of 18 card sets, 100 WAiNK locations, all 33 underwater discoveries and 100 of 125 cocaine flamingos; 42 shooting range gold medals, 42 drone time trials, 300 stunts and 70 of 80 challenges. This stage earns A Long Road (Gold) and most of the Silvers.

Trophies: A Long Road (Gold), the grind Silvers, and most of the activity trophies.

Stage 3 — Clean up 10–20 hours

Finish the eight qualifying Strangers & Freaks strands (Andy’s, Billy’s and Tyrone’s do not count — they have their own trophies), the five side-story finales for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, the last meditations, and any trophy still open. Then Vice Got a Price! pops — the Platinum is yours.

Trophies: the five finales, Strangers & Freaks (Silver), the last meditations, then the Platinum.

⚠ The one missable trophy

Positive Mental Attitude (Bronze) — meditate at all 18 spots across Leonida. One spot is Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1: after the mission Down to Earth, find Lucia at Jason’s house in the morning, follow her to the balcony and accept. It is gone once Chapter 1 ends, and there is no mission replay. Do it before you leave the chapter and keep a Chapter 1 manual save until the trophy pops. Everything else in the game can be done after the story.

Also time-sensitive, but not trophy-missable: the Race Against Yago in Chapter 3 carries an hourglass icon — race it the moment it appears, because its strand counts toward Strangers & Freaks.
